'''Fianlimab''' (development code '''REGN3767''') is an investigational fully human [[monoclonal antibody]] that targets [[lymphocyte-activation gene 3]] (LAG-3), an [[immune checkpoint]] receptor expressed on [[T cell]]s. It is being developed by [[Regeneron Pharmaceuticals]], primarily in combination with the [[anti-PD-1]] antibody [[cemiplimab]], as a [[cancer immunotherapy]] for [[melanoma]] and other solid tumours.<ref name="jco2024">{{cite journal |vauthors=Hamid O, Lewis KD, Weise A, McKean M, Papadopoulos KP, Crown J |display-authors=etal |title=Phase I Study of Fianlimab, a Human Lymphocyte Activation Gene-3 (LAG-3) Monoclonal Antibody, in Combination With Cemiplimab in Advanced Melanoma |journal=Journal of Clinical Oncology |volume=42 |issue=24 |pages=2928–2938 |date=20 August 2024 |pmid=38900987 |doi=10.1200/JCO.23.02172}}</ref>

== Mechanism of action ==
Fianlimab is a fully human [[immunoglobulin G]] monoclonal antibody that binds LAG-3, an inhibitory immune checkpoint receptor. LAG-3 signalling, driven by interaction with [[MHC class II]], suppresses T-cell activation, and its expression on tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes is associated with resistance to [[PD-1]]-directed therapy. By blocking LAG-3, fianlimab is intended to restore T-cell activity; in preclinical models its antitumour effect was enhanced when combined with PD-1 blockade.<ref name="mct2019">{{cite journal |title=Preclinical Development of the Anti-LAG-3 Antibody REGN3767: Characterization and Activity in Combination with the Anti-PD-1 Antibody Cemiplimab in Human PD-1×LAG-3–Knockin Mice |journal=Molecular Cancer Therapeutics |volume=18 |issue=11 |pages=2051–2062 |date=November 2019 |pmid=31395688 |doi=10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-18-1376}}</ref>

== Development ==
REGN3767 was generated by Regeneron using its [[VelocImmune]] platform, which produces fully human antibodies in genetically modified mice. Preclinical characterisation, published in 2019, showed high-affinity binding to LAG-3 and antitumour activity in combination with cemiplimab in mice engineered to carry the human extracellular domains of PD-1 and LAG-3, supporting the start of clinical trials.<ref name="mct2019" />

== Clinical trials ==
=== Phase 1 ===
In a first-in-human dose-escalation and expansion study, fianlimab combined with cemiplimab was evaluated in patients with advanced malignancies. In patients with advanced melanoma who had not previously received anti-PD-1 or anti-PD-L1 therapy, the combination produced objective responses in more than 60% of patients, with durable responses reported across high-risk subgroups.<ref name="jco2024" /><ref name="regeneron2022">{{cite press release |title=Fianlimab (LAG-3 Inhibitor) Combined with Libtayo (cemiplimab) Demonstrates Greater than 60% Response Rates in Two Independent Cohorts of Patients with Advanced Melanoma Naïve to PD-1 or PD-L1 Inhibitors |publisher=Regeneron Pharmaceuticals |url=https://investor.regeneron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fianlimab-lag-3-inhibitor-combined-libtayor-cemiplimab/ |access-date=14 May 2026}}</ref>

=== Phase 3 ===
Regeneron has advanced fianlimab plus cemiplimab into [[Phase 3 clinical trial|phase 3]] testing in melanoma. Trials include a study comparing the combination with [[pembrolizumab]] in previously untreated, unresectable, locally advanced or metastatic melanoma ({{ClinicalTrialsGov|NCT05352672|link=no}}), and a study comparing it with the [[nivolumab/relatlimab]] combination in the same setting ({{ClinicalTrialsGov|NCT06246916|link=no}}). A separate phase 3 trial is evaluating the combination as adjuvant therapy in resected high-risk melanoma.<ref name="mra2026">{{cite web |title=Clinical Trials Testing Combination Immunotherapy for High-Risk and Advanced Melanoma: Regeneron's LAG-3 Program |publisher=Melanoma Research Alliance |url=https://www.curemelanoma.org/blog/clinical-trials-testing-combination-immunotherapy-for-high-risk-and-advanced-melanoma-regenerons-lag-3-program |access-date=14 May 2026}}</ref> The combination is also being studied in a phase 2/3 trial in first-line advanced [[non-small-cell lung cancer]].<ref name="mra2026" />

In its first-quarter 2026 results, Regeneron stated that an Independent Data Monitoring Committee had recommended that the phase 3 adjuvant melanoma study continue as planned, and that pivotal data for the first-line metastatic melanoma indication were anticipated in the second quarter of 2026.<ref name="regeneron2026q1">{{cite press release |title=Regeneron Reports First Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results |publisher=Regeneron Pharmaceuticals |url=https://investor.regeneron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/regeneron-reports-first-quarter-2026-financial-and-operating/ |access-date=14 May 2026}}</ref>
