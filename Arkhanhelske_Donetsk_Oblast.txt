'''Arkhanhelske''' ({{langx|uk|Архангельське}}; [[Russian language|Russian]]: Архангельское) is a village in [[Ocheretyne settlement hromada]], [[Pokrovsk Raion]], [[Donetsk Oblast]], eastern [[Ukraine]]. As of 2001, the population was 285 people.

==History==
Russia claimed to have taken control over the village during its [[Russian invasion of Ukraine|invasion of Ukraine]] on 25 May 2024.<ref>{{cite news |title=Russia Says Captured Another Village in East Ukraine |url=https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2024/05/25/russia-says-captured-another-village-in-east-ukraine-a85223 |access-date=25 May 2024|work=The Moscow Times |date=25 May 2024 |language=en}}</ref> The occupation was confirmed by [[DeepStateMap.Live]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://deepstatemap.live/en#6/48.2685691/37.6391602|title=Arkhanhelske, Donetsk Oblast|website=[[DeepStateMap.Live]]}}</ref>

== Geography ==
The distance from Arkhanhelske to the district centre is approximately 29&nbsp;km.

== Notable people ==
Literary critic {{ill|Dmytro Vasylovych Chaliy|uk|Чалий Дмитро Васильович}} was born here.
