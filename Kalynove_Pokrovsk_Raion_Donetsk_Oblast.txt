''' Kalynove''' ({{langx|uk|Калинове}}; {{langx|ru|link=no|Калиново}}) is a village in [[Novohrodivka urban hromada]], [[Pokrovsk Raion]], [[Donetsk Oblast]], eastern [[Ukraine]]. It is located {{convert|31.04|km|mi}} northwest by north ([[Points of the compass#32-wind compass rose|NWbN]]) from the centre of [[Donetsk]] city.

==History==
The settlement was founded in 1885.

===Russian invasion of Ukraine===
The village was captured by Russian Forces in August 2024, during the [[Russian invasion of Ukraine|full-scale Russian invasion of Ukraine]].<ref name=ISW_2024-08-27 />

==Demographics==
As of the [[2001 Ukrainian census]], the settlement had 1,192 inhabitants, whose [[First language|native languages]] were 74.89% [[Ukrainian language|Ukrainian]] and 25.11% [[Russian language|Russian]].<ref name=ukrcensus />
