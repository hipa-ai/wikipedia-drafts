A '''broker's price opinion''' (BPO) is a report that is performed by a licensed [[real estate agent]], [[broker]], or appraiser.  A BPO is an informal appraisal.  It is similar to a CMA ([[Comparative Market Analysis]]), though the real estate professional is usually paid to prepare a BPO. A BPO can be either an exterior drive-by or a full interior report. When doing a BPO, the real estate professional researches the subject property, takes pictures of it, investigates the neighborhood, as well as retrieves six comparable properties (three Active Comps and three Sold Comps) in their MLS ([[Multiple Listing Service]]). The final BPO is used to support their professional opinion that will help determine the potential selling price<ref name=NABPOP-overview/> or estimated value<ref name=Investopedia-overview/> of a property.

==Involved parties==
Broker price opinions are initiated by financial institutions.<ref name=NABPOP-overview/> Examples are [[bank]]s, [[mortgage]] companies, and [[loss mitigation]] companies.<ref name=About.com-overview/><ref name=About-when>{{cite web | url = http://realestate.about.com/od/lendingandmortgages/qt/bpo_used_when.htm | title = When is a BPO or Broker Price Opinion Used?: Who Might Call You for a BPO and Why | first = James | last = Kimmons | publisher = About.com | accessdate = February 28, 2015 | archive-date = 2 April 2015 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150402105249/http://realestate.about.com/od/lendingandmortgages/qt/bpo_used_when.htm | url-status = dead }}</ref><ref name=About-twotypes>{{cite web | url = http://realestate.about.com/od/appraisalandvaluation/qt/bpo_2_types.htm | title = The Two Major Types of BPO or Broker Price Opinions: What Are Drive By and Internal BPO's? | first = James | last = Kimmons | publisher = About.com | accessdate = February 28, 2015 | archive-date = 2 April 2015 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150402141716/http://realestate.about.com/od/appraisalandvaluation/qt/bpo_2_types.htm | url-status = dead }}</ref>

The BPO is performed by a real estate professional who is acting on behalf of the financial institution.<ref name=NABPOP-overview/> Such a professional could be a [[real estate agent]], a [[Real estate agent#The difference between salespersons and brokers|real estate broker]], an [[appraiser]], or other qualified person.<ref name=Investopedia-overview/><ref name=NABPOP-overview/> Through BPOs, real estate professionals can improve their skills in property inspection, market knowledge, evaluation, and property pricing.<ref name=NABPOP-curious>{{cite web | url = http://nabpop.org/CuriousBpo.php | title = Income doing Broker Price Opinions – BPOs; Increase Skills as a Real Estate Professional | publisher = [[National Association of Broker Price Opinion Professionals]] (NABPOP) | accessdate = February 28, 2015}}</ref> At a price of US$30–$100 per BPO,<ref name=About-twotypes/> the work can provide side income or steady income for real estate agents.<ref name=NABPOP-overview/> Agents may also create working relationships and a rapport with financial institutions.<ref name=NABPOP-overview/><ref>{{cite book | first1 = William L. Jr. | last1 = Ventolo | first2 = Martha R. | last2 = Williams | title = The Art of Real Estate Appraisal: Dollars and Cents Answers to Your Questions | year = 1992 | publisher = Dearborn Financial Publishing Inc. | isbn = 0-79310-207-3 | type = Paperback | pages = [https://archive.org/details/artofrealestatea0000vent/page/25 25–73] | url = https://archive.org/details/artofrealestatea0000vent/page/25 }}</ref> Some BPO agents work through a BPO company that provides a single point of contact to the client and oversight of agents and their reports.<ref name=NABPOP-overview/>

==Uses of a BPO==
A financial institution may order a BPO for:
* situations that do not require the expense or time of a full appraisal<ref name=About-when/><ref name=NABPOP-overview/><ref name=NAR-embrace/>
* [[real estate owned]] (REO) properties<ref name=NABPOP-overview/>
* pending foreclosures<ref name=About-when/> or [[foreclosure]]d properties<ref name=NABPOP-overview/>
* [[Short (finance)|short sales]]<ref name=NABPOP-overview/>
* an addition or a cross-check to an appraisal
* [[home equity loan]]s<ref name=NABPOP-overview/> or a [[home equity line of credit]]<ref name=NABPOP-overview/> of less than $250,000{{citation needed|date=February 2015}}
* [[refinancing]]<ref name=About-when/>
* appeal to cancel [[lenders mortgage insurance|lender's mortgage insurance]] (LMI or PMI)<ref name=NABPOP-overview/>
* [[due diligence]] by financial institutions<ref name=NABPOP-overview/>
* [[asset]] evaluation and [[bookkeeping]] by financial institutions<ref name=NABPOP-overview/>
* a selling or purchase price for an individual property<ref name=CFPB/> or a portfolio of home loans (sometimes for thousands of loans)<ref name=NABPOP-overview/>

==Overview of the BPO process==
A real estate agent or a broker is hired to complete a BPO report on a property. A BPO may be occasionally requested without a fee in hopes that the financial institution, bank, or lender will receive a sales listing for the property.<ref name=NABPOP-overview>{{cite web | title = BPO Defined | url = http://nabpop.org/New-BPO.php | publisher = National Association of Broker Price Opinion Professionals (NABPOP) | accessdate = February 28, 2015}}</ref>

The BPO professional then conducts the investigation. Many factors affect the price of a property:
* characteristics of the individual property, such as structure type, size, room use, age, lot size, and parking<ref name=About-driveby>{{cite web | url = http://realestate.about.com/od/appraisalandvaluation/qt/drive_by_bpo.htm | title = What is Involved in a Drive By BPO or Broker Price Opinion? The Drive By BPO is a Bit More Than the Name Implies | first = James | last = Kimmons | publisher = About.com | accessdate = February 28, 2015 | archive-date = 2 April 2015 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150402122343/http://realestate.about.com/od/appraisalandvaluation/qt/drive_by_bpo.htm | url-status = dead }}</ref><ref name=About-internal>{{cite web | url = http://realestate.about.com/od/appraisalandvaluation/qt/internal_bpo_do.htm | title = What Is Involved in an Internal BPO or Broker Price Opinion? | first = James | last = Kimmons | publisher = About.com | accessdate = February 28, 2015 | archive-date = 2 April 2015 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150402121721/http://realestate.about.com/od/appraisalandvaluation/qt/internal_bpo_do.htm | url-status = dead }}</ref>
* how the property fits into the immediate neighborhood<ref name=About-driveby/>
* compliance with local [[zoning]] requirements<ref name=About-driveby/>
* characteristics of the neighborhood<ref name=About-driveby/>
* values of similar properties in the surrounding area<ref name=Investopedia-overview/>
* the amount of cleaning, repair, and preparation needed to make the property marketable<ref name=Investopedia-overview>{{cite web | title = Broker Price Opinion – BPO |url=http://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/broker_price_opinion.asp | work = Investopedia | publisher = Investopedia, LLC | accessdate = February 28, 2015}}</ref><ref name=About-internal/>
* BPO and comparative market analysis (CMA) can be provided by real estate brokers to establish a general idea of what a home could be listed for in the current real estate market.

The result is a brief report, usually two or three pages in length. This report includes analysis of the property and neighborhood, photographs, [[Sales comparison approach|comparable properties]] ("comps"), and local and regional market information.<ref name=NABPOP-overview/><ref name=About-driveby/>

==Types of BPOs==
A broker may do a drive-by BPO or an interior BPO.<ref name=About.com-overview>{{cite web | title = All About The BPO or Broker Price Opinion | first = James | last = Kimmons | url = https://www.topproducers-realestate.com/od/appraisalandvaluation/tp/od-appraisalandvaluation-tp-bpo-basics/ | publisher = About.com | accessdate = 3 July 2011 }}</ref> In drive-by BPOs, the professionals do not have access to the interior of the property;<ref name=About-twotypes/> they rely on exterior appearance, neighborhood information, comparables, and other documentation.<ref name=About-driveby/> The interior BPO is more in-depth; it requires the agent to make contact with the borrower or point of contact (POC) to schedule an inspection, and when arriving the agent looks the property over and notes all repairs needed to bring the property to average condition fair market value (updates and upgrades are not allowed, unless the agent can show that the local market calls for updates or upgrades i.e. granite countertops stainless appliances and hardwood floors). A photo of the front, rear and both sides of exterior are taken, as well as a photo of each room of the interior, as well as all mechanicals and appliances. When completing the report, value is chosen for 30 day quicksale, and 60–90 day values, and a suggested [[list price]] for each is calculated based on market conditions and list to sale ratio for that market. The repaired fair market value is then reduced by itemized repair costs to find the as-is value.<ref name=About-twotypes/><ref name=About-internal/>

==Comparison to other kinds of value analysis==
;Automated valuation model
In an [[automated valuation model]] (AVM), a computerized mathematical model evaluates basic features of the property, compares to area sales, and determines a rough value. The calculation takes into account information about property size, bedrooms, and bathrooms. An AVM is a basic evaluation – "just to make sure that the property value is in the right ball park" – and may be replaced by a full real estate appraisal.<ref name=CFPB>{{cite web | url = http://www.consumerfinance.gov/askcfpb/1875/during-my-mortgage-loan-application-process-i-was-given-three-or-four-different-valuations-what-do-all-the-different-valuations-mean.html | title = During my mortgage loan application process, I was given three or four different valuations. What do all the different valuations mean? | date = May 5, 2014 | work = Ask CFPB | publisher = [[Consumer Financial Protection Bureau]] | accessdate = February 28, 2015}}</ref> Because an AVM does not involve a site visit, it does not include factors such as condition or upgrades.<ref name=NAR-embrace>{{cite press release | url = http://www.realtor.org/news-releases/2014/11/realtors-should-embrace-online-data-educate-consumers-about-valuations | title = Realtors Should Embrace Online Data, Educate Consumers about Valuations | date = November 8, 2014 | location = New Orleans | publisher = [[National Association of Realtors]] | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150402122454/http://www.realtor.org/news-releases/2014/11/realtors-should-embrace-online-data-educate-consumers-about-valuations | archive-date = 2 April 2015 | url-status = dead}}</ref>

;Comparative Market Analysis
An individual who is looking to sell or purchase a home might request that an agent perform a comparative market analysis (CMA)<ref name=NAR-embrace/> to determine a reasonable listing price or offering price.<ref>{{cite web | url = http://www.realtor.org/appraisal/responsible-valuation-policy | title = Responsible Valuation Policy | date = February 16, 2012 | publisher = National Association of Realtors}}</ref> The process of deciding a CMA price is similar to a BPO.<ref name=NABPOP-overview/> However, a BPO – whether the exterior drive-by BPO or the full interior BPO – is more thorough than most CMAs.<ref name=About-nabpop>{{cite web | url = http://realestate.about.com/od/appraisalandvaluation/qt/bpo_nabpop.htm | title = NABPOP – The National Association of Broker Price Opinion Professionals: A Professional Association with Training and Certification | first = James | last = Kimmons | publisher = About.com | accessdate = February 28, 2015 | archive-date = 2 April 2015 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150402090650/http://realestate.about.com/od/appraisalandvaluation/qt/bpo_nabpop.htm | url-status = dead }}</ref>

;Real estate appraisal
Unlike a BPO professional, a [[real estate appraiser]] must be licensed or certified in most countries. A BPO is less thorough than an appraisal.{{citation needed|date=February 2015}}

Only a licensed or certified appraiser can execute a valuation or an appraisal.<ref>{{cite web | title = Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP) | publisher = [[Appraisal Foundation|The Appraisal Foundation]] | url = https://www.appraisalfoundation.org/imis/TAF/Standards/Appraisal_Standards/Uniform_Standards_of_Professional_Appraisal_Practice/TAF/USPAP.aspx }}</ref>
