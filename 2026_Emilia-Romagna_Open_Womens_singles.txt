[[Mayar Sherif]] was the defending champion,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tennistourtalk.com/122203/sherif-secures-second-parma-ladies-open-title|title=Sherif Secures Second Parma Ladies Open Title|publisher=tennistourtalk.com|accessdate=17 May 2025}}</ref> but lost in the second round to [[Dominika Šalková]].

[[Dayana Yastremska]] won the title, after defeating [[Barbora Krejčíková]] in the final, 6–3, 6–3.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tenisportal.cz/zpravy/titul-byl-daleko-krejcikova-ve-finale-nestacila-titul-z-parmy-slavi-jastremska-56654/|title=Titul byl daleko.
