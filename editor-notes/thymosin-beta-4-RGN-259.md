# Thymosin beta-4 / RGN-259 — editor notes

**File:** `wikipedia-addition-thymosin-beta-4-RGN-259.txt`
**Type:** Section add-on — NOT a new article.

## Why it is not a new article
"RGN-259" is an eye-drop formulation of **thymosin beta-4** (INN: timbetasin),
an endogenous peptide that already has a Wikipedia article:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thymosin_beta-4

A standalone "RGN-259" page would get merged into it. This content goes into
the existing article instead.

## What to do
1. Open https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thymosin_beta-4 → "Edit source".
2. Find the `== Clinical significance ==` section. It currently has a single
   sentence about dry eye and neurotrophic keratopathy.
3. Paste the `=== Ophthalmic drug development (RGN-259) ===` subsection
   **immediately after that sentence**, inside the Clinical significance
   section.
4. Edit summary suggestion: "Expand Clinical significance with RGN-259
   ophthalmic drug-development history".

## Important — tone
RGN-259 has **mixed-to-negative** trial results: the dry-eye phase 3 program
(ARISE) missed its co-primary endpoints, and a European phase 3 trial in
neurotrophic keratitis (SEER-3) missed its primary endpoint. The draft states
this plainly and that is correct — **do not soften or remove the negative
results.** Wikipedia requires balanced coverage; making it sound like a
success would get the edit reverted.
