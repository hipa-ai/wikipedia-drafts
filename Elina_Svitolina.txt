'''Elina Svitolina''' ({{langx|uk|Еліна Світоліна}}, {{IPA|uk|eˈl⁽ʲ⁾inɐ s⁽ʲ⁾wiˈtɔl⁽ʲ⁾inɐ|pron}};<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-nF3p4gF28o&t=574 |archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/varchive/youtube/20211215/-nF3p4gF28o |archive-date=2021-12-15 |url-status=live |title=🎾Элина Свитолина про первый секс, 500$ в час, самолёт в подарок и ругань матом – YouTube |website=[[YouTube]] |date=9 September 2018 |access-date=2020-09-20}}{{cbignore}}<br />{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0wNdIRHUxx0&t=18 |archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/varchive/youtube/20211215/0wNdIRHUxx0 |archive-date=2021-12-15 |url-status=live |title=Ексклюзивне інтерв'ю з найуспішнішою тенісисткою Світоліною та її коханим – YouTube |website=[[YouTube]] |date=21 September 2019 |access-date=2020-09-20}}{{cbignore}}</ref> born 12 September 1994) is a Ukrainian professional [[tennis]] player. She has career-high rankings of world No. 3 in singles and No. 108 in doubles by the [[Women's Tennis Association|WTA]]. Svitolina has won 19 [[WTA Tour]] singles titles, including the [[2018 WTA Finals – Singles|2018 WTA Finals]] and four [[WTA Premier tournaments|Premier 5]]-level tournaments, and has reached four [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] semifinals.

Svitolina first broke into the world's top 50 in July 2013 and the top 10 in May 2017, making her the [[List of highest ranked tennis players per country|highest-ranked Ukrainian woman]] in history. Svitolina reached two major semifinals in 2019 at [[2019 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|Wimbledon]] and the [[2019 US Open – Women's singles|US Open]]. In 2021, she won an Olympic bronze medal in the [[Tennis at the 2020 Summer Olympics – Women's singles|women's singles tournament]] at the [[2020 Tokyo Olympics]] to become the first Olympian to win a medal in tennis for Ukraine.

In 2022, Svitolina took a break from professional tennis to give birth to her first child. She made a strong comeback upon her return to competition in 2023, reaching the [[2023 French Open – Women's singles|French Open]] quarterfinals and the [[2023 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|Wimbledon]] semifinals in her first few tournaments back. She achieved consistent success in the following years, culminating in an [[2026 Australian Open – Women's singles|Australian Open]] semifinal and return to the world's top 10 in 2026.

==Early life and background==
Svitolina was born in [[Odesa]] to [[Ukrainians|Ukrainian]] parents, Mikhaylo Svitolin (a former [[wrestler]]) and Olena Svitolina (a former competitive [[rowing (sport)|rower]]).<ref name="I'm from Ukraine"/> She has a [[Jews|Jewish]] grandmother.<ref>{{cite web |title=Свитолина созналась, что ее бабушка — еврейка |url=https://ukrsport.info/46443/svitolina-soznalas-chto-ee-babushka-evrejka/ |website=ukrsport.info |date=12 January 2022 |language=ru |last1=Яценко |first1=Максим |access-date=14 July 2023 |archive-date=14 July 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230714011405/https://ukrsport.info/46443/svitolina-soznalas-chto-ee-babushka-evrejka/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> She was named after the famous Soviet actress [[Elina Bystritskaya]]. ==Career==
===Early career===
Yulian Svitolin began full time coaching career in 2003 as a head coach of his younger sister, Elina Svitolina.
Yulian Svitolin worked with Elina for 5 years. She was 14 years old at that time and made a tremendous improvement and break through at the age of 15 by winning the Grand Slam, Roland Garros. Svitolina's greatest achievement as a junior was winning the [[2010 French Open|French Open]] girls event in 2010, beating Tunisian [[Ons Jabeur]] in the final.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.rolandgarros.com/en_FR/news/match_reports/2010-06-06/201006061275844275835.html |title=Svitolina and Velotti take French Open junior titles |work=[[French Open|Roland Garros]] |date=June 6, 2010 |access-date=August 18, 2010 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100811065423/http://www.rolandgarros.com/en_FR/news/match_reports/2010-06-06/201006061275844275835.html |archive-date=August 11, 2010}}</ref> She reached her first professional singles final at the [[2010 ITF Women's Circuit|$25k]] tournament in [[Kharkiv]] in May 2010.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.itftennis.com/womens/tournaments/printabledrawsheet.asp?tournament=1100021716&event=1100123537 |title=$25,000 Kharkiv Ukraine Women's Singles – Main Draw |work=[[International Tennis Federation]] |access-date=August 18, 2010}}</ref> She also reached the final of the girls' singles event at the [[2012 Wimbledon Championships]], where she lost to [[Eugenie Bouchard]].

Svitolina made her [[WTA Tour]] main-draw debut at the [[2012 Baku Cup – Singles|2012 Baku Cup]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Tales from Sopot, Portoroz, Baku, Florianopolis and Nanchang |publisher=Women's Tennis Association |date=2020-07-30 | url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/1745753/future-stars-and-swansongs-at-sopot-portoroz-baku-florianopolis-and-nanchang | accessdate = 2025-10-03}}</ref> She qualified for the [[2012 US Open (tennis)|US Open]] and was defeated in the first round by 12th seed and eventual quarterfinalist [[Ana Ivanovic]]. She won the [[WTA 125 tournaments|WTA 125]] [[2012 Royal Indian Open|Royal Indian Open]] title in [[Pune]], defeating [[Andreja Klepač]], [[Rutuja Bhosale]], [[Luksika Kumkhum]], former top-10 player [[Andrea Petkovic]], and Japanese veteran [[Kimiko Date|Kimiko Date-Krumm]] in the final.

===2013: WTA Tour title===
Svitolina gained direct entry into the [[2013 Australian Open|Australian Open]], where she was defeated by fifth seed [[Angelique Kerber]] in the first round. She won her first WTA title at the [[2013 Baku Cup|Baku Cup]] by beating [[Shahar Pe'er]]; in doing so, Svitolina became the first teenager to win a WTA tournament since February 2012.<ref name=UPTES28713>[http://www.upi.com/Sports_News/2013/07/28/Elina-Svitolina-picks-up-first-WTA-title-at-Baku-Cup/UPI-21211375012505/ Elina Svitolina picks up first WTA title at Baku Cup], [[United Press International]] (28 July 2013)</ref> The victory led to a jump of 32 spots in the WTA rankings, landing her at No. 49 on 29 July 2013.<ref>[http://www.upi.com/Sports_News/2013/07/29/Svitolina-rising-quickly-in-WTA-rankings/UPI-78041375102279/ Svitolina rising quickly in WTA rankings], [[United Press International]] (29 July 2013)</ref>

===2014: Ascent, second career title===
At the [[2014 Australian Open|Australian Open]], Svitolina defeated two-time Grand Slam champion and three-time Australian Open quarterfinalist [[Svetlana Kuznetsova]] in the opening round in straight sets.<ref>[http://azstarnet.com/sports/tennis/serena-earns-record-tying-th-victory-at-australian-open/article_a7d3708e-9119-5539-8888-523e08b75f69.html Serena earns record-tying 60th victory at Australian Open], ''Arizona Daily Star'', 15 January 2014</ref> She went on to reach the third round, losing to [[Sloane Stephens]] in straight sets.

After defending her Baku Cup title by beating [[Bojana Jovanovski]] in the final, Svitolina played at the [[2014 Western & Southern Open|Western & Southern Open]] where she recorded the first top-ten victory of her career, defeating recently crowned [[2014 Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]] champion [[Petra Kvitová]] in the second round.<ref>[http://www.wtatennis.com/news/article/4072531/title/kuznetsova-stuns-bouchard-in-cincy Kuznetsova stuns Bouchard in Cincy], WTA official website, 13 August 2014</ref> She proceeded to reach her first quarterfinal at Premier-5 level, eventually losing to Ana Ivanovic in straight sets.<ref>[http://www.wtatennis.com/news/article/4077035/title/sharapova-wins-halep-showdown Sharapova wins Halep showdown], WTA official website, 15 August 2014</ref>

Svitolina reached her first Premier-5 semifinal in the first edition of the WTA tournament in [[Wuhan Open|Wuhan]], defeating [[Camila Giorgi]], [[Sabine Lisicki]], [[Garbiñe Muguruza]] via walkover, and Angelique Kerber before losing to Petra Kvitová in the semifinals.

===2015: First major quarterfinal, No. 15===
[[File:Svitolina RG15 (2) (19118955970).jpg|thumb|upright=.7|right|Svitolina at the [[2015 French Open]]]]
{{BLP unreferenced section|date=April 2022}}
She began the new season at the [[2015 Brisbane International|Brisbane International]] where she reached the semifinals before losing to eventual champion [[Maria Sharapova]]. At the [[2015 Australian Open|Australian Open]], Svitolina reached the third round, and won the first set against [[List of WTA number 1 ranked singles players|world No. 1]] and eventual champion, Serena Williams, before losing in three sets.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dnaindia.com/sport/report-serena-williams-fends-off-elina-svitolina-to-reach-fourth-round-of-australian-open-2055289 |title=Serena Williams fends off Elina Svitolina to reach fourth round of Australian Open |publisher=DNA India |date=24 January 2015 |access-date=24 January 2015}}</ref> She fell in the second round in both [[2015 Dubai Tennis Championships|Dubai]] and [[2015 Qatar Total Open|Doha]], losing in tight three-set matches to Petra Kvitová and [[Victoria Azarenka]], respectively.

As the 23rd seed in [[2015 BNP Paribas Open|Indian Wells]], she defeated [[Alison Van Uytvanck]] and [[Lucie Šafářová]], before losing in the fourth round to [[Timea Bacsinszky]]. The following week in [[2015 Miami Open|Miami]], she defeated Bojana Jovanovski before losing in the third round to the eighth seed [[Ekaterina Makarova]]. As the top seed at a tournament for the first time in [[2015 Copa Colsanitas|Bogotá]], Svitolina reached the semifinals, defeating [[Louisa Chirico]], [[Danka Kovinić]] and [[Irina Falconi]], before losing to eventual champion [[Teliana Pereira]].

She continued her successful start to the clay-court season by winning her third career title and first on clay in [[2015 Grand Prix SAR La Princesse Lalla Meryem|Marrakech]], recovering from 2–5 down in the opening set to defeat [[Tímea Babos]], in straight sets. The victory propelled her to a career high ranking of 21. She also reached the semifinals in doubles with compatriot [[Olga Savchuk]]. The following week in [[2015 Mutua Madrid Open|Madrid]], after easily dispatching [[Daniela Hantuchová]], Svitolina lost to Ana Ivanovic for the sixth time in her career. A similar scenario occurred in [[2015 Italian Open (tennis)|Rome]], where she defeated [[Flavia Pennetta]] in straight sets before losing to [[Venus Williams]].

At the [[2015 French Open – Women's singles|French Open]], after comfortably seeing off [[Yanina Wickmayer]] in her opening match, Svitolina had to recover from 0–3 in the second set and 1–4 in the final set to beat [[Yulia Putintseva]] in a match that lasted over three hours. Another tight three-set victory ensued in the third round against [[Annika Beck]], before Svitolina defeated [[Alizé Cornet]] in a rain-interrupted fourth-round encounter to set up a maiden Grand Slam quarterfinal against seventh seed Ana Ivanovic, which she lost in straight sets. Svitolina rose to No. 17 after this tournament, surpassing [[Alona Bondarenko]] as the highest-ranked Ukrainian woman in the Open Era, including women from Ukraine who played under the Soviet Union.

Svitolina's grass-court season was significantly less successful, losing in three sets in the second round of [[2015 Aegon International|Eastbourne]] to [[Heather Watson]]. As the 17th seed at [[2015 Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]], she came from a set down to beat [[Misaki Doi]], who had beaten her at the same stage the previous year, before again losing in the second round to Australian [[Casey Dellacqua]]. Despite losing in the first round of [[2015 İstanbul Cup|İstanbul]] to [[Magdaléna Rybáriková]], Svitolina successfully defended her doubles title from the previous year, this time partnering [[Daria Gavrilova]].

She began U.S. hardcourt season with a run to the semifinals in [[2015 Bank of the West Classic|Stanford]], losing in straight sets to eventual champion Angelique Kerber. At the [[2015 Rogers Cup|Rogers Cup]], Svitolina lost in the first round to Victoria Azarenka. She bettered her performance from the previous year in [[2015 Western & Southern Open|Cincinnati]], defeating [[Alison Riske]], Eugenie Bouchard and [[Caroline Garcia]] to reach the quarterfinals, before defeating Lucie Šafářová for the second time this year to progress to her second Premier-5 semifinal, where she lost to Serena Williams. Svitolina concluded her warm up for the [[2015 US Open (tennis)|US Open]] in [[2015 Connecticut Open|New Haven]], where she retired from her first-round match against [[Madison Keys]]. She progressed to the third round of the [[2015 US Open (tennis)|US Open]], defeating [[Elizaveta Kulichkova]] and [[Kaia Kanepi]], before losing to 13th seed Ekaterina Makarova for the third time this year.

The Asian hardcourt swing began in [[2015 Toray Pan Pacific Open|Tokyo]] for Svitolina, where she lost in the second round to eventual champion [[Agnieszka Radwańska]]. Svitolina failed to repeat her success from the previous year in [[2015 Wuhan Open|Wuhan]], losing in the third round to [[Karolína Plíšková]]. Similarly disappointing results ensued for Svitolina, losing in the second round of [[2015 China Open (tennis)|Beijing]] to [[Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova]], and the first round of [[2015 Tianjin Open|Tianjin]] to [[Elena Vesnina]].

Despite this disappointing end to her season, Svitolina's consistency throughout the year earned her a spot as the eighth seed at the inaugural [[WTA Elite Trophy]] in Zhuhai. She was drawn into Group B with second seed [[Carla Suárez Navarro]] and Andrea Petkovic. Svitolina topped the group by winning both her matches, advancing to the semifinals where she lost to Karolína Plíšková. Her last match of the season was against [[Pauline Parmentier]] in the first round of the [[WTA 125 tournaments|WTA 125 tournament]] in [[2015 Open de Limoges|Limoges]], which she lost on a final-set tiebreak.

===2016: Olympic quarterfinal===
[[File:Elina Svitolina (25170599953).jpg|thumb|left|upright=.7|Svitolina at the 2016 Indian Wells Open]]
Svitolina began her season at the [[2016 Hopman Cup|Hopman Cup]], representing Ukraine with [[Alexandr Dolgopolov]]. She was undefeated in her singles matches in the round-robin stage, defeating [[Victoria Duval]], Karolína Plíšková, and [[Jarmila Wolfe]]. Svitolina and Dolgopolov were the winners of their group, and advanced to the final, where the Australian pairing of [[Nick Kyrgios]] and Daria Gavrilova defeated them 2–0, with Svitolina losing her singles match in straight sets. At the [[2016 Apia International Sydney|Sydney International]], Svitolina lost in the first round to Angelique Kerber. In [[2016 Australian Open|Melbourne]], she defeated Victoria Duval in straight sets, before losing to qualifier [[Naomi Osaka]] in the second round.

In her first tournament with Justine Henin acting as a coaching consultant, Svitolina advanced to the semifinals in [[2016 Dubai Tennis Championships|Dubai]], defeating qualifier [[Jana Čepelová]], earning her first top-10 win of the year over second seed Garbiñe Muguruza,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dubaidutyfreetennischampionships.com/en/news-and-media/news/2016/02/17/dubai-2016-wta-wed-evening-report |title=Ivanovic Topples Top Seed Halep at Dubai Duty Free Tennis Championships<!-- Bot generated title --> |access-date=19 February 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160524091256/http://www.dubaidutyfreetennischampionships.com/en/news-and-media/news/2016/02/17/dubai-2016-wta-wed-evening-report |archive-date=24 May 2016 |url-status=dead}}</ref> and battling past [[CoCo Vandeweghe]] in three sets, before losing to eventual champion [[Sara Errani]]. Svitolina subsequently lost in the first round in [[2016 Qatar Total Open|Doha]] to [[Denisa Allertová]].

Her next tournament was the [[Malaysian Open (tennis)|Malaysian Open]] where she defeated [[Miyu Kato (tennis)|Miyu Kato]], [[Risa Ozaki]], [[Kristína Kučová]], and [[Zhu Lin (tennis)|Zhu Lin]] en route to the final where she beat a resurgent Eugenie Bouchard in a rain-interrupted marathon to win her fourth WTA Tour title. This victory saw her maintain her perfect record in WTA finals, as well as improving her ranking to a career high of 14.

At the [[2016 BNP Paribas Open|Indian Wells Open]], 17th-seed Svitolina defeated Annika Beck, before losing in straight sets to ninth-seed [[Roberta Vinci]]. Then, in Miami, after defeating Australian Open quarterfinalist [[Zhang Shuai]], Svitolina scored one of the biggest victories of her career, coming from a break down in the third set to defeat former [[List of WTA number 1 ranked tennis players|WTA No. 1]], [[Caroline Wozniacki]], in three sets in the third round. Svitolina subsequently lost in the fourth round to Ekaterina Makarova, who beat her at the same tournament the prior year.

Svitolina began her clay-court season with a disappointing first-round loss to [[Alexandra Panova]] in [[2016 Copa Colsanitas|Bogotá]], followed by a second-round loss to Daria Gavrilova in [[2016 Mutua Madrid Open|Madrid]], and a first-round loss to qualifier [[Monica Puig]] in [[2016 Italian Open (tennis)|Rome]]. Entering the [[2016 French Open|French Open]] in poor form, Svitolina beat Romanian qualifier [[Sorana Cîrstea]] and wildcard [[Taylor Townsend]] before beating nemesis and former world No. 1, Ana Ivanovic, in straight sets.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.rolandgarros.com/en_FR/news/articles/2016-05-28/svitolina_ends_former_champions_run.html |title=Svitolina ends former champion's run |website=Roland Garros |access-date=24 July 2016}}</ref> She then lost in the fourth round to world No. 1, defending champion, and eventual finalist Serena Williams.

In [[2016 Aegon Classic Birmingham|Birmingham]], she lost in the first round to Carla Suárez Navarro. At Wimbledon, she beat [[Naomi Broady]] in straight sets before being upset by [[Yaroslava Shvedova]].

At the [[Tennis at the 2016 Summer Olympics|Rio Olympics]], Svitolina scored her first win over defending champion and then-world No. 1, Serena Williams, in the third round to reach the quarterfinals, defeating the out-of-sorts American in straight sets. Svitolina failed to follow up her landmark victory in the next round, however, losing to eventual bronze medalist Petra Kvitová.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.abc.net.au/news/2016-08-10/rio-2016-samantha-stosur-serena-williams-out-of-tennis-singles/7712628?section=sport |title=Rio 2016: Australian Samantha Stosur, top-ranked Serena Williams out of Olympic women's singles |work=ABC News ([[Australian Broadcasting Corporation]]) |date=10 August 2016 |access-date=10 August 2016}}</ref>

American hardcourt season began with a run to the third round of [[2016 Rogers Cup|Montréal]], losing to Angelique Kerber. After a disappointing early loss to Daria Gavrilova in [[2016 Western & Southern Open|Cincinnati]], she rebounded at [[2016 Connecticut Open|New Haven]], reaching the final before losing to Agnieszka Radwańska. At the [[2016 US Open (tennis)|US Open]], Svitolina reached the third round for the second consecutive year with wins over [[Mandy Minella]] and [[Lauren Davis]], but she lost to Petra Kvitová once again.

The Asian swing proved to be fruitful for Svitolina, reaching the semifinals of both [[2016 Toray Pan Pacific Open|Tokyo]], where she was defeated by Naomi Osaka, and [[2016 China Open (tennis)|Beijing]], her first semifinal at Premier-Mandatory level, losing to eventual champion Agnieszka Radwańska. As a result, her ranking was propelled back up to 15, one place shy of her career high, and guaranteed her a place in [[2016 WTA Elite Trophy|Zhuhai]] for the second year running. Her good form continued at the last Premier event of the season in [[2016 Kremlin Cup|Moscow]], where she reached the semifinals, her third in four tournaments, before bowing out to eventual champion Svetlana Kuznetsova.

Svitolina concluded her season as the fourth seed in Zhuhai. She topped her group by defeating [[Kiki Bertens]] and Elena Vesnina, and then came from a set down to defeat top seed [[Johanna Konta]] in the semifinals to advance to her third final of the year, where she lost to Kvitová. Svitolina finished the season ranked 14th, with an impressive 13–5 win–loss record following the US Open.

===2017: Three Premier 5 titles, No. 3===
[[File:Elina Svitolina (33318618651).jpg|thumb|right|upright=.7|Svitolina at the [[2017 BNP Paribas Open|2017 Indian Wells Open]]]]
{{BLP unreferenced section|date=April 2022}}
Svitolina began her 2017 season with a strong showing in [[2017 Brisbane International|Brisbane]], defeating world No. 1, Angelique Kerber, in the quarterfinals, before losing to eventual champion Karolína Plíšková in the following round. The victory over Kerber signified her third win over a number-one-ranked player in five months. Svitolina was seeded 11th at the [[2017 Australian Open|Australian Open]], her highest seeding to date. She equalled her best showing in Melbourne, reaching the third round with victories over [[Galina Voskoboeva]] and [[Julia Boserup]], before losing to Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova.

Svitolina won her fifth career title at the [[2017 Taiwan Open|Taiwan Open]], fending off four match points in her quarterfinal encounter with Ons Jabeur, before defeating Peng Shuai in the final. Svitolina next competed in Ukraine's [[2017 Fed Cup World Group II|Fed Cup]] tie against Australia, where she won both of her singles rubbers to help Ukraine set up a play-off against Germany. Svitolina continued her strong start to the season in [[2017 Dubai Tennis Championships|Dubai]], where she defeated Caroline Wozniacki in straight sets in the final to capture her first title at Premier 5-level. This win propelled her into the WTA top 10 for the first time in her career, making her the first Ukrainian ever to do so. Svitolina failed to carry her momentum into the American hard-court swing, losing in the fourth round of [[2017 BNP Paribas Open|Indian Wells]] to Garbiñe Muguruza, and the second round of [[2017 Miami Open|Miami]] to [[Bethanie Mattek-Sands]].

She began the clay-court season by capturing her third title of the year in [[2017 İstanbul Cup|İstanbul]]. Rebounding from a disappointing loss to [[Zheng Saisai]] in the first round of [[2017 Mutua Madrid Open|Madrid]], Svitolina then proceeded to reach her second Premier-5-level final of the year, and fourth overall, in [[2017 Italian Open (tennis)|Rome]]. After defeating two top-5 players en route in Karolína Plíšková and Garbiñe Muguruza, she faced off against [[Simona Halep]], whom she defeated in three sets to claim the title. The win propelled her to a career high of No. 6 in the singles rankings, as well as to the top spot in the Race to Singapore. She then started her campaign at the [[2017 French Open|French Open]] as one of the favourites to win the tournament. Svitolina progressed through her first three matches with relative ease, defeating Yaroslava Shvedova, [[Tsvetana Pironkova]] and [[Magda Linette]] respectively. Svitolina then overcame qualifier [[Petra Martić]] in a tight three set encounter to advance to her second major quarterfinal. She faced Simona Halep in a rematch of the [[2017 Italian Open (tennis)|Italian Open]] final. Svitolina led by a set and 5–1 and held a match point against the 2014 finalist, but lost in three sets (the third set at love).

At [[2017 Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]], she surpassed her previous performances by reaching the fourth round, defeating Birmingham finalist and potential dark horse [[Ashleigh Barty]] in the first round. She then eased past [[Francesca Schiavone]] and [[Carina Witthöft]], before bowing out to 13th seed and French Open champion [[Jeļena Ostapenko]] in straight sets.

Her fifth title of the year, and third successive at Premier 5-level, came in [[2017 Rogers Cup|Toronto]], where she defeated four top-10 players en route in Venus Williams, Garbiñe Muguruza, Simona Halep and Caroline Wozniacki. Svitolina's first loss of the season at Premier-5-level came the following week in the third round of [[2017 Western & Southern Open|Cincinnati]], losing to [[Julia Görges]].

She entered the [[2017 US Open (tennis)|US Open]] knowing that a run to the semifinals would see her claim the [[List of WTA number 1 ranked tennis players|world-number-one ranking]] for the first time. She overcame [[Kateřina Siniaková]] in a rain-interrupted three-set encounter, before easing past [[Evgeniya Rodina]] and [[Shelby Rogers]] to reach the round of 16 at the US Open for the first time. She was then defeated by eventual runner-up [[Madison Keys]] in three sets.

Despite missing a large part of the Asian swing, Svitolina still secured her place at the year-end [[2017 WTA Finals|WTA Finals]] in Singapore, making her the first Ukrainian ever to do so. As the third seed in [[2017 China Open (tennis)|Beijing]], she progressed to her eighth quarterfinal of the year with relative ease, defeating wildcard Zhu Lin, recent Wuhan finalist Ashleigh Barty and Elena Vesnina. There, she was defeated by Caroline Garcia on a final-set tiebreak.

Svitolina was drawn into the Red Group at the WTA Finals, alongside Halep, Wozniacki and Caroline Garcia. Despite earning her fifth career win over a number-one-ranked player by defeating Halep, Svitolina lost her other two matches, including a two-set drubbing by eventual champion Wozniacki, which ultimately placed her third in the group. She finished the year ranked at No. 6.

===2018: WTA Finals champion===
[[File:Svitolina RG18 (86) (42260263064).jpg|thumb|right|upright=.7|Svitolina at the [[2018 French Open]]]]
Svitolina began her 2018 season by capturing the [[2018 Brisbane International|Brisbane International]] title, defeating qualifier [[Aliaksandra Sasnovich]] in the final.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Chiesa |first1=Victoria |title=Svitolina stops Sasnovich, wins first title in Brisbane |work=WTA Tennis |url=http://www.wtatennis.com/news/svitolina-stops-sasnovich-wins-first-title-brisbane |date=6 January 2018 |access-date=8 January 2018}}</ref> She then competed at the [[2018 Australian Open|Australian Open]] as the fourth seed, faced qualifier [[Ivana Jorović]] and won in straight sets. Against Kateřina Siniaková, Svitolina prevailed in three sets. She then had two straight-sets wins over qualifier and compatriot [[Marta Kostyuk]] and [[Denisa Allertová]] to advance to her first Australian Open quarterfinal where she was upset by [[Elise Mertens]] in straight sets.<ref>{{cite web |title=Magical Mertens stuns Svitolina to make Aussie Open semis |url=http://www.wtatennis.com/news/magical-mertens-stuns-svitolina-make-aussie-open-semis |date=23 January 2018 |access-date=24 January 2018}}</ref>

Svitolina's 11th career title came after a successful defence of her crown in [[2018 Dubai Tennis Championships|Dubai]], where she brushed aside [[Daria Kasatkina]] in the final. After losing to Carla Suárez Navarro in the third round of [[2018 BNP Paribas Open|Indian Wells]], she produced her best ever result in [[2018 Miami Open|Miami]], reaching the quarterfinals before falling to eventual runner-up Jeļena Ostapenko in straight sets.

Rebounding from a second-round loss in [[2018 Mutua Madrid Open|Madrid]] to Suárez Navarro, Svitolina claimed her second straight [[2018 Italian Open (tennis)|Italian Open]] crown, defeating top seed Simona Halep in two sets, in a rematch of the previous year's final.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Juzwiak |first1=Jason |title=Svitolina slides to second straight Rome title over Halep |work=WTA Tennis |url=http://www.wtatennis.com/news/svitolina-slides-second-straight-rome-title-over-halep |date=20 May 2018 |access-date=2 June 2018}}</ref> At the [[2018 French Open|French Open]], Svitolina posted wins against [[Ajla Tomljanović]] and [[Viktória Kužmová]], before being upset in the third round by [[Mihaela Buzărnescu]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Juzwiak |first1=Jason |title=Buzarnescu blows by Svitolina in stunning Paris upset |work=WTA Tennis |url=http://www.wtatennis.com/news/buzarnescu-blows-svitolina-stunning-paris-upset |date=1 June 2018 |access-date=2 June 2018}}</ref>

She began her grass-court season with a run to the quarterfinals of [[2018 Birmingham Classic|Birmingham]], losing again to Buzărnescu.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Chiesa |first1=Victoria |title=Buzarnescu bounces Svitolina from Birmingham |work=WTA Tennis |url=http://www.wtatennis.com/news/buzarnescu-bounces-svitolina-birmingham |date=22 June 2018 |access-date=23 June 2018}}</ref> Svitolina was then upset in the first round of [[2018 Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]] by recent [[2018 Mallorca Open|Mallorca]] champion [[Tatjana Maria]].

She enjoyed moderate success during the [[2018 US Open Series|US Open Series]], reaching the semifinals in [[2018 Rogers Cup|Montreal]], where she was the defending champion, and the quarterfinals of [[2018 Western & Southern Open|Cincinnati]], losing there to eventual champion Kiki Bertens. She reached the round of 16 at the [[2018 US Open (tennis)|US Open]] for the second straight year, losing to [[Anastasija Sevastova]] in three sets.

Svitolina's struggles with form continued into the Asian swing, with back-to-back opening round losses to eventual champion [[Aryna Sabalenka]] in [[2018 Wuhan Open|Wuhan]], and [[Aleksandra Krunić]] in [[2018 China Open (tennis)|Beijing]], despite opening up a 6–0, 3–0 lead in the latter match. A quarterfinal defeat in [[2018 Hong Kong Tennis Open|Hong Kong]] to in-form [[Wang Qiang (tennis)|Wang Qiang]], coupled with Svitolina's decision not to play in the final week of the year at either [[2018 Kremlin Cup|Moscow]] or [[2018 BGL Luxembourg Open|Luxembourg City]], meant that qualification for the WTA Finals in Singapore would rest on the performances of Karolína Plíšková and Kiki Bertens in Moscow. Plíšková's defeat in the second round ensured that Svitolina would qualify for Singapore for the second successive year.

As the sixth seed, she was drawn into the White Group, alongside defending champion Caroline Wozniacki, and Czechs Plíšková and Petra Kvitová. She won all three of her round-robin matches, first snapping a seven-match losing streak against Kvitová, defeating her in straight sets. She then defeated both Plíšková and Wozniacki in three sets to secure her place in the semifinals alongside Plíšková. She defeated Kiki Bertens in three sets, and in the final, she came from a set down to beat Sloane Stephens, winning the biggest title of her career. Svitolina ended the year as the world No. 4.

===2019: Two major semifinals===
Svitolina opened the season with an unsuccessful title defense in [[2019 Brisbane International|Brisbane]]; she lost her first match to the previous year's finalist, Aliaksandra Sasnovich.

Seeded sixth at the [[2019 Australian Open|Australian Open]], she defeated qualifier [[Viktorija Golubic]], Viktória Kužmová, Zhang Shuai, and 17th seed Madison Keys to make her second consecutive quarterfinal at the Australian Open. She was, however, afflicted by a neck and shoulder injury and was defeated by fourth seed and eventual champion Naomi Osaka in straight sets. Svitolina then reached back-to-back semifinals in [[2019 Qatar Total Open|Doha]], losing with the lead in the final set against Simona Halep, and [[2019 Dubai Tennis Championships|Dubai]], where she was the two-time defending champion, losing in a third-set tiebreak to eventual champion Belinda Bencic.

Svitolina reached her third consecutive semifinal of the year at [[2019 BNP Paribas Open – Women's singles|Indian Wells]]. In her first Premier Mandatory semifinal since 2016, she lost in three sets to 18-year-old wildcard and eventual champion [[Bianca Andreescu]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Andreescu surprises Svitolina to reach biggest final of career at Indian Wells |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/andreescu-surprises-svitolina-reach-biggest-final-career-indian-wells |work=WTA Tennis |date=16 March 2019 |access-date=24 March 2019}}</ref> The next week at the [[2019 Miami Open – Women's singles|Miami Open]], she suffered a surprise defeat in her opening match against [[Wang Yafan]].<ref>{{cite web |title=In-form Wang Yafan springs Svitolina surprise in Miami |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/form-wang-yafan-springs-svitolina-surprise-miami |work=WTA Tennis |date=23 March 2019 |access-date=24 March 2019}}</ref> Svitolina later revealed that she had been struggling with knee pain for several weeks and announced she would take a short break to recover.

Svitolina's clay-court season began with back-to-back opening round losses to Pauline Parmentier in [[2019 Mutua Madrid Open|Madrid]] and to Victoria Azarenka in [[2019 Italian Open (tennis)|Rome]], where she was the two-time defending champion and had held a match point leading in the final set. At the [[2019 French Open – Women's singles|French Open]], Svitolina was defeated in the third round by the 2016 champion, Garbiñe Muguruza.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/muguruza-slides-past-svitolina-paris-fourth-round |title=Muguruza slides past Svitolina into Paris fourth round |last=Juzwiak |first=Jason |date=31 May 2019 |website=WTA Tennis |access-date=31 May 2019}}</ref>

[[File:Elina Svitolina (49745028971).jpg|thumb|right|upright=.7|Svitolina at the 2019 Wimbledon Championships]]
After losing in her opening round at both [[2019 Birmingham Classic|Birmingham]] and [[2019 Eastbourne International|Eastbourne]], Svitolina proceeded to reach her first Grand Slam semifinal at [[2019 Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]] with victories over Daria Gavrilova, [[Margarita Gasparyan]], [[Maria Sakkari]], Petra Martić and Karolína Muchová, becoming the first Ukrainian ever to do so.<ref>{{cite news |title=Svitolina reaches first career Grand Slam semi-final with victory over Muchova |url=https://asia.eurosport.com/tennis/wimbledon-women/2019/wimbledon-2019-elina-svitolina-v-karolina-muchova-live-text-updates_sto7363432/story.shtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190709161918/https://asia.eurosport.com/tennis/wimbledon-women/2019/wimbledon-2019-elina-svitolina-v-karolina-muchova-live-text-updates_sto7363432/story.shtml |url-status=dead |archive-date=July 9, 2019 |access-date=9 July 2019 |work=Eurosport |date=9 July 2019}}</ref> There, she faced eventual champion Simona Halep and was defeated in two sets.

The North American hardcourt season began for Svitolina at [[2019 Silicon Valley Classic|San Jose]], where as the top seed, she fell to Maria Sakkari in the quarterfinals. She would then fall to [[Sofia Kenin]] in both the quarterfinals of [[2019 Rogers Cup|Toronto]] and the round of 16 at [[2019 Western & Southern Open|Cincinnati]]. Entering the [[2019 US Open (tennis)|US Open]] as the No. 5 seed, Svitolina defeated [[Whitney Osuigwe]], Venus Williams, [[Dayana Yastremska]] and Madison Keys to reach her first US Open quarterfinal. There, she defeated Johanna Konta in straight sets for a spot in her second successive Grand Slam semifinal. She was then defeated in straight sets by Serena Williams. She subsequently returned to her career-high ranking position of No. 3.

Svitolina reached three consecutive quarterfinals in the season-closing Asian swing, losing to [[Kristina Mladenovic]] in [[2019 Zhengzhou Open|Zhengzhou]], eventual runner-up Alison Riske in [[2019 Wuhan Open|Wuhan]], and Kiki Bertens in [[2019 China Open (tennis)|Beijing]]. Despite failing to reach a final during the season, Svitolina's overall consistency managed to secure her spot as the eighth seed at the WTA Finals in [[2019 WTA Finals|Shenzhen]] for the third successive year. She was drawn into the Purple Group alongside Karolína Plíšková, US Open champion Bianca Andreescu, who was later replaced by Sofia Kenin, and Wimbledon champion Simona Halep. Svitolina topped her group, winning all three matches without dropping a set. Her semifinal match against Belinda Bencic was cut short when Bencic retired with a leg injury down 4–1 in the final set, meaning that Svitolina would advance to the final for the second year in succession, as well as her first final since winning the title in 2018. She failed to defend her title, however, losing in straight sets to Ashleigh Barty. This meant Svitolina failed to win a title during the year for the first time since 2012.

On 31 March 2022, Svitolina announced that she was taking a temporary break from competition, citing health problems, including back pain, and emotional exhaustion from the [[Russian invasion of Ukraine]].<ref name="624563baedda6SVITOLINA">{{cite news |title="THE BODY CAN NO LONGER STAND": SVITOLINA ANNOUNCED A TEMPORARY PAUSE IN HIS SPORTS CAREER |url=https://tabloid.pravda.com.ua/person/624563baedda6/ |access-date=31 March 2022 |work=[[Tablo ID]] |date=31 March 2022 |language=uk}}</ref> In May, she later announced that she was pregnant with her first child,<ref>{{Cite web |date=15 May 2022 |title=Elina Svitolina, Gael Monfils announce pregnancy |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2617412/elina-svitolina-gael-monfils-announce-pregnancy |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230304125209/https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2617412/elina-svitolina-gael-monfils-announce-pregnancy |archive-date=4 March 2023 |access-date=17 March 2023 |publisher=[[Women's Tennis Association]]}}</ref> after which she gave birth in October.<ref>{{Cite web |date=15 October 2022 |title=Elina Svitolina, Gael Monfils announce birth of first child |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2852506/elina-svitolina-gael-monfils-announce-birth-of-first-child |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221028153843/https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2852506/elina-svitolina-gael-monfils-announce-birth-of-first-child |archive-date=28 October 2022 |access-date=17 March 2023 |publisher=[[Women's Tennis Association]]}}</ref>

Throughout her hiatus, Svitolina took on an active role in charity exhibitions and galas to raise awareness and money in support of Ukraine. In July, she joined Iga Świątek and [[Agnieszka Radwańska]] for an exhibition in [[Kraków]] in which she was the chair umpire; together, they raised over [[euro|€]]400,000 for her eponymous foundation, [[United24]], and [[:pl:Polski Komitet Narodowy UNICEF|UNICEF Polska]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=29 June 2022 |title=Iga Swiatek will host charity tennis event in Poland to benefit children impacted by war in Ukraine |url=https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/34166682/iga-swiatek-host-charity-tennis-event-poland-ukrainian-children |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220817071552/https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/34166682/iga-swiatek-host-charity-tennis-event-poland-ukrainian-children |archive-date=17 August 2022 |access-date=17 March 2023 |website=[[ESPN]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=23 July 2022 |title=Photo of the Day: Swiatek rallies for Radwanska's return in charity exhibition |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2688477/photo-of-the-day-swiatek-rallies-for-radwanska-s-return-in-charity-exhibition |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221225231411/https://www.wtatennis.com/news/2688477/photo-of-the-day-swiatek-rallies-for-radwanska-s-return-in-charity-exhibition |archive-date=25 December 2022 |access-date=17 March 2023 |publisher=[[Women's Tennis Association]]}}</ref> In October, she raised over [[euro|€]]240,000 for her foundation and United24 in a charity gala in [[Monaco]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=6 December 2022 |title=Elina Svitolina raises over $250K in support of Ukraine with charity gala in Monaco |url=https://www.tennis.com/baseline/articles/elina-svitolina-raises-250k-in-support-of-ukraine-with-charity-gala-in-monaco |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221209212006/https://www.tennis.com/baseline/articles/elina-svitolina-raises-250k-in-support-of-ukraine-with-charity-gala-in-monaco |archive-date=9 December 2022 |access-date=17 March 2023 |website=[[Tennis (magazine)|Tennis]]}}</ref>

She reached the fourth round at the [[2023 French Open|French Open]] defeating 26th seed [[Martina Trevisan]], along with comeback wins over qualifier [[Storm Hunter]], and again over Anna Blinkova. She went on to defeat the ninth seed Daria Kasatkina to book her spot in a Grand Slam quarterfinal for the ninth time in her career, her fourth at the French Open and her first at a major since the US Open in 2021.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/rg2023-svitolina-kasatkina-fourth-round-report |title=Quarter-Final Beyond Svitolina's Wildest Dreams |publisher=Roland Garros |last=Imhoff |first=Dan |date=4 June 2023 |access-date=16 June 2023}}</ref> Despite losing to world No. 2 and reigning Australian Open champion, Aryna Sabalenka, in straight sets, Svitolina returned to the top 100 on 12 June 2023.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/3535256/rankings-watch-haddad-maia-breaks-into-top-10-svitolina-climbs-higher |title=Rankings Watch: Haddad Maia breaks into Top 10; Svitolina climbs higher |publisher=WTA |date=12 June 2023 |access-date=16 June 2023}}</ref>

Her good form continued at [[2023 Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]] where she reached back to back quarterfinals as a wildcard defeating [[Venus Williams]], Elise Mertens, Sofia Kenin and 19th seed Victoria Azarenka. She defeated the world No. 1, Iga Świątek, to make her second Wimbledon semifinal.<ref>{{Cite news |date=10 July 2023 |title=Watch Wimbledon: Svitolina knocks out top seed Swiatek to reach semis |language=en-GB |work=BBC Sport |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/live/tennis/65584745 |access-date=11 July 2023}}</ref> It was also her fourth win over a major champion.
She became the third player in the [[Open Era]] to defeat four former major champions in a single Grand Slam tournament after Serena Williams at the 1999 US Open and Justine Henin at Roland Garros in 2005.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/3586809/svitolina-stuns-swiatek-in-wimbledon-quarterfinals |title=Svitolina stuns Swiatek in Wimbledon quarterfinals}}</ref> As a result, she returned to the top 30 rising close to 50 positions up in the rankings.

In July 2023 when Ukrainian four-time individual world sabre champion [[Olga Kharlan]] was disqualified at the [[2023 World Fencing Championships|World Fencing Championship]]s for not shaking the hand of her defeated Russian opponent, though she instead offered a tapping of blades in acknowledgement, Svitolina voiced support for Kharlan.<ref name="auto1a">{{Cite web |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/fencing/66322668 |title=World Fencing Championships: Ukraine's Olga Kharlan disqualified for refusing Russian Anna Smirnov's handshake |work=BBC |date=27 July 2023}}</ref><ref name="auto3a">{{Cite web |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/fencing-ukraine-russia-handshake-world-championship-b2383066.html |title=Ukrainian fencer disqualified from world championships for refusing handshake with Russian opponent; Olga Kharlan offered to touch blades after beating Anna Smirnova, who then staged a sit-down protest at the handshake refusal |date=27 July 2023 |website=The Independent |author=Aadi Nair}}</ref>  "Why don't Russians respect our position?" she asked.<ref name="auto1a"/> She called on the [[Fédération Internationale d'Escrime]] to follow the WTA's lead; it has supported Svitolina's decision to not shake hands with Russians or Belarusians after matches.<ref name="auto2">{{Cite web |url=https://www.theguardian.com/world/2023/jul/27/ukraine-calls-for-disqualified-fencer-to-be-reinstated-after-anti-russia-protest |title=Ukraine calls for disqualified fencer to be reinstated after anti-Russia protest |first=Julian |last=Borger |date=27 July 2023 |work=The Guardian}}</ref> She said "I think the other sports federations should do the same. They should respect our decision, and the decision of our country as well."<ref name="auto2"/> Svitolina called the FIE's disqualification "disrespectful" towards Ukrainians.<ref name="auto2"/>

===2024–25: Three major quarterfinals===
At the [[2024 Australian Open]], Svitolina reached the fourth round, but retired with a back injury in her match against [[Linda Nosková]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2024/jan/22/australian-open-2024-elina-svitolina-withdrawal-injury |title='Shot me in the back': Elina Svitolina's cruel injury sparks delicious Australian Open chaos |work=The Guardian |date=22 January 2024 |accessdate=29 January 2024 |first=Tumaini |last=Carayol}}</ref>

At the [[2024 French Open]], she advanced to the fourth round, where she lost to [[Elena Rybakina]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=de La Hamaide |first1=Sybille |title=Rybakina marches past Svitolina into French Open quarter-finals |url=https://www.reuters.com/sports/tennis/rybakina-marches-past-svitolina-into-french-open-quarter-finals-2024-06-03/ |website=Reuters| date=3 June 2024}}</ref> Seeded 21st at [[2024 Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]], she defeated world No. 10, [[Ons Jabeur]], in the third round<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4052761/svitolina-takes-down-two-time-finalist-jabeur-at-wimbledon |title=Svitolina takes down two-time finalist Jabeur at Wimbledon |publisher=WTA |accessdate=8 July 2024}}</ref> and [[Wang Xinyu]] in round four.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.tennismajors.com/wimbledon-news/wimbledon-svitolina-into-quarter-finals-772495.html |title=Svitolina cruises into quarter-finals vs. Rybakina |date=8 July 2024 |publisher=Tennis Majors |accessdate=8 July 2024}}</ref> In the quarterfinals, she once again lost to Rybakina in straight sets.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4055340/rybakina-cruises-past-svitolina-into-wimbledon-semifinals |title=Rybakina cruises past Svitolina into Wimbledon semifinals |work=Women's Tennis Association |publisher=WTA |accessdate=10 July 2024}}</ref>

Svitolina was a flag bearer for Ukraine at the [[2024 Summer Olympics]] in Paris. As a defending bronze medalist, she upset fifth seed [[Jessica Pegula]] in the second round.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Imhoff |first1=Dan |title=At Olympics, Svitolina competing for something bigger than most |publisher=Australian Open|url=https://ausopen.com/articles/news/olympics-svitolina-competing-something-bigger-most |date=30 July 2024}}</ref> She lost to reigning Wimbledon champion [[Barbora Krejčíková]] in the third round.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Malachy |first1=Jamie |title=Krejcikova ends Svitolina's Olympic dream in Paris, but Kostyuk battles through |url=https://www.tennismajors.com/olympic-games/krejcikova-ends-svitolinas-olympic-dream-in-paris-but-kostyuk-battles-through-776369.html |website=Tennis Majors |date=30 July 2024}}</ref>

Seeded 27th at the [[2024 US Open (tennis)|2024 US Open]], she lost to defending champion [[Coco Gauff]] in the third round.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Maciel |first1=Claire |title=Coco Gauff tops Svitolina to keep title defense alive at 2024 US Open |url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2024-08-30/coco_gauff_tops_svitolina_to_keep_title_defense_alive_at_2024_us_open.html |publisher=US Open |date=30 August 2024}}</ref> She ended her 2024 season early at the end of September following her withdrawal due to surgery from the [[2024 China Open (tennis)|China Open]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.sportskeeda.com/tennis/8-wta-players-will-miss-china-open-2024-ft-iga-swiatek-danielle-collins |title=8 WTA players who will miss China Open 2024 ft. Iga Swiatek, Danielle Collins |date=21 September 2024|publisher=Sportskeeda}}</ref>

With her second-round win over [[Caroline Dolehide]] at the [[2025 Australian Open]], Svitolina became the first Ukrainian player, male or female, in the [[Open Era]] with 100 main-draw wins at Grand Slam tournaments. She subsequently reached the quarterfinals with wins over fourth seed [[Jasmine Paolini]] and former world No. 9, [[Veronika Kudermetova]], for the third time at the tournament.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4200858/svitolina-turns-tables-to-upset-paolini-at-australian-open-keys-advances |title=Svitolina turns tables to upset Paolini at Australian Open; Keys advances |work=Women's Tennis Association |date=18 January 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4201358/keys-ousts-rybakina-in-melbourne-sets-quarterfinal-meeting-with-svitolina |title=Keys ousts Rybakina in Melbourne; sets quarterfinal meeting with Svitolina |date=21 January 2025}}</ref> In the quarterfinals, she lost to eventual champion [[Madison Keys]] in three sets.<ref>{{cite web |title=Keys wins 10th straight match, tops Svitolina to make Australian Open semis |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4202275/keys-wins-10th-straight-match-tops-svitolina-to-make-australian-open-semis |website=Women's Tennis Asssociation|date=22 January 2025}}</ref>

Svitolina had a successful clay season in 2025. She defeated [[Olga Danilović]] to win her eighteenth WTA title at the [[2025 Open de Rouen – Singles|Open de Rouen]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Top seed Svitolina triumphs in Rouen; wins first title in nearly two years |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4251946/top-seed-svitolina-triumphs-in-rouen-wins-first-title-in-nearly-two-years |website=Women's Tennis Association|date=20 April 2025}}</ref> Seeded 17th at the [[2025 Mutua Madrid Open|Madrid Open]], she defeated [[Sonay Kartal]], 10th seed Rybakina, [[Maria Sakkari]], and [[Moyuka Uchijima]] to extend her win streak to ten matches.<ref>{{cite web |title=Madrid Open: Svitolina ends Uchijima's run to reach semi-finals |url=https://www.tennismajors.com/wta-tour-news/madrid-mutua-open-wta-1000/madrid-open-svitolina-ends-uchijimas-run-to-reach-semi-finals-819728.html |website=Tennis Majors|date=30 April 2025}}</ref> She lost to eventual champion [[Aryna Sabalenka]] in the semifinals.<ref>{{cite web |title=Sabalenka ends Svitolina's winning run to return to the Madrid Open final |url=https://www.flashscore.com/news/tennis-madrid-wta-singles-sabalenka-ends-svitolina-s-winning-run-to-return-to-the-madrid-open-final/h298GM3j/ |website=Flashscore|date=1 May 2025}}</ref> She followed that up with a quarterfinal appearance at [[2025 Italian Open|Rome]], losing to [[Peyton Stearns]] despite a third set lead.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Mesic |first1=Dzevad |title=Rome: Elina Svitolina fails to return to SF after blowing big third-set lead |url=https://www.tennisworldusa.org/tennis/news/On_the_WTA_results_with/155600/rome-elina-svitolina-fails-to-return-to-sf-after-blowing-big-thirdset-lead/ |website=Tennis World|date=14 May 2025}}</ref> At the [[2025 French Open|French Open]], she defeated reigning finalist Paolini in the fourth round to make her second major quarterfinal of the year. There, she lost to three-time defending champion [[Iga Świątek]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Swiatek soars to set up Sabalenka semi |url=https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/rg2025-swiatek-soars-to-set-up-sabalenka-blockbuster |website=Roland Garros|date=3 June 2025|first1=Alex|last1=Sharp}}</ref>

Seeded 14th at [[2025 Wimbledon Championships|Wimbledon]], Svitolina lost to [[Elise Mertens]] in the third round.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Reid |first1=Candy |title=Mertens too strong for Svitolina |url=https://www.wimbledon.com/en_GB/news/articles/2025-07-04/mertens_too_strong_for_svitolina.html|website=The Championships, Wimbledon|date=4 July 2025}}</ref> She reached the quarterfinals in [[2025 Canadian Open|Canada]] but lost in the first round of [[2025 Cincinnati Open|Cincinnati]] and the [[2025 US Open (tennis)|US Open]]. After losing in the semifinals of the [[2025 Billie Jean King Cup finals|Billie Jean King Cup]] to [[Jasmine Paolini]], Svitolina ended her season early, citing her need to heal and recharge.<ref>{{cite web |title=Svitolina, Haddad Maia will skip the rest of the 2025 season |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4368116/elina-svitolina-beatriz-haddad-maia-skip-rest-of-season |website=Women's Tennis Association|date=23 September 2025}}</ref> She finished the year ranked No. 14, her highest finish since her return.

==Playing style==
[[File:Roland Garros 2015 - Elina Svitolina (23478056595).jpg|thumb|right|upright=.7|Svitolina returning from the baseline at the [[2015 French Open]]]]
Svitolina is a defensive baseliner. ''[[The Guardian]]'' described Svitolina's playing style as "deceptively casual", but being capable of "brilliant displays" when in good form.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2018/oct/28/elina-svitolina-wta-finals-sloane-stephens-tennis |title=Elina Svitolina's defensive masterclass seals WTA Finals win against Stephens |work=[[The Guardian]] |date=28 October 2018 |access-date=28 December 2020}}</ref> Svitolina is capable of turning defense into offense when given the chance, and is capable of hitting low-risk winners from defensive positions. Her groundstrokes are very consistent and are hit with moderate power, although she is capable of hitting groundstrokes with significant power when the opportunity arises. Her backhand is her stronger side, especially down the line and on the run, although both her forehand and her backhand penetrate deep into the court. Her second serve is particularly weak and often exploited allowing return winners. She often struggles if playing against someone who is very aggressive and not making many unforced errors on that day.<ref name=Matchfacts>{{cite web |work=wtatour.com |title=WTA MATCHFACTS |url=http://wtafiles.wtatennis.com/pdf/rankings/WTAMatchStats.pdf |access-date=8 April 2018}}</ref> Her first serve speed peaks at {{convert|110|mph|km/h|abbr=on|round=5}}, allowing her to serve aces. She has been known to struggle with volleying at the net, but she possesses good reactions, and she can accelerate well across the court to deal with short balls. Her favourite surfaces are [[grass court|grass]] and [[clay court|clay]],<ref name="ITF"/> although she is yet to reach a singles final on grass.

==Coaches==
Svitolina was coached by Sébastien Mathieu in 2013 and 2014. She then worked with Iain Hughes for two and a half years, and chose to split from him in November 2016.<ref name="ITF"/><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.wtatennis.com/news/article/6076287/title/svitolina-splits-with-coach-iain-hughes |title=Svitolina Splits With Coach Iain Hughes |website=Women's Tennis Association |access-date=16 November 2016}}</ref> In February 2016, she added former world No. 1 [[Justine Henin]] to her coaching team.<ref>[http://www.wtatennis.com/news/article/5340234/title/henin-to-help-rising-svitolina "Henin to help rising Svitolina." Women's Tennis Association.] Published February 11, 2016.</ref> In late 2016, Svitolina announced that [[Gabriel Urpí]] would be her new coach, with [[Thierry Ascione]] replacing Henin as her new coaching consultant.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.wtatennis.com/news/article/6150706/title/svitolina-announces-new-coaching-team-led-by-gabriel-urpi |title=Svitolina Announces New Coaching Team, Led By Gabriel Urpi |website=Women's Tennis Association |access-date=13 December 2016}}</ref> Ascione coached Svitolina until the 2018 US Open, and she then had a brief stint with [[Nick Saviano]], whilst still being accompanied on a weekly basis by hitting partner Andrew Bettles. Bettles became her full-time coach for the 2018 season, and former player [[Marcos Baghdatis]] began working with her for the 2020 season. Following the end of her 2021 season, Svitolina split with Bettles,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kane |first=David |date=25 October 2021 |title=Elina Svitolina announces split from longtime coach Andrew Bettles |url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/elina-svitolina-announces-split-from-longtime-coach-andrew-bettles |url-status=live |access-date=13 December 2021 |website=[[Tennis (magazine)|Tennis]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211025205947/https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/elina-svitolina-announces-split-from-longtime-coach-andrew-bettles |archive-date=25 October 2021}}</ref> announcing her new team would be made of [[Andis Juška]] as new coach and [[Günter Bresnik|Gunter Bresnik]] as consultant.<ref>{{Cite web |date=23 December 2021 |title=Elina Svitolina announces her new coaching team for 2022 |url=https://www.topfivemanagement.net/elina-svitolina-new-coaching-team-2022e |work=topfivemanagement }}{{Dead link|date=October 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref> After her maternity leave hiatus in 2022, Svitolina returned to the tour in April 2023 with [[Raemon Sluiter]].<ref name=":0" /> Svitolina announced she had split from Sluiter on 19 July 2024.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4062771/svitolina-announces-split-with-coach-raemon-sluiter |title=Svitolina announces split with coach Raemon Sluiter |publisher=WTA |accessdate=22 July 2024}}</ref>
